# Microsoft official device mockups — confirmed absent

**Microsoft does not publicly distribute official mockups of its own hardware, Surface
included.**

Routes checked:

- **The official Figma account @microsoft** — it publishes the Windows UI Kit, the
  Microsoft 365 UI Kit and some 30 Fluent kits and plugins, but **no hardware mockups**
  (see [`figma-community-kits.md`](figma-community-kits.md)).
- **Web search** — everything that turns up for "Surface mockup PSD" is third party
  (unblast, psdrepo, Anthony Boyd and others). There is no official distribution channel.
- Microsoft's brand site covers logo and trademark guidelines only.

## Unverified

- There may be partner-only marketing assets in the Microsoft Partner Center — it requires a
  login, so this is **unverified**.
- Press product photography is in the news.microsoft.com image gallery, but it is for press
  use.

## Alternatives

If you need a Surface mockup:

- **Meta Devices** ([`meta-devices.md`](meta-devices.md)) — includes Surface 3 · Surface
  Pro 4 · Surface Pro X · Surface Book · Lumia 950. Non-commercial use only.
- Otherwise, third-party kits — each licence has to be checked individually.
