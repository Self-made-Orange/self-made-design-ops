# Devices (Design at Meta) — inventory

Formerly Facebook Design's "Devices". **It has not been discontinued** —
`design.facebook.com/tools/devices` 301-redirects to
`meta.com/design-at-meta/tools/devices/`, and the downloads are live.

It is **the only large official (corporate-published) collection** that offers devices from
many manufacturers in one place. The traps are that the publisher is Meta rather than the
manufacturers, and that the licence is narrow.

- URL: <https://www.meta.com/design-at-meta/tools/devices/>
- A single bulk download (`Devices.zip`) plus per-device zips
- The format inside the zip was **not verified** by opening one — per the old
  facebook.design notes it is Sketch plus PNG
- Licence: <https://www.meta.com/design-at-meta/license/>

## When it was last updated

The newest devices are the **iPhone 13 series / Galaxy S21 series / Pixel 5 / Surface Pro X**
— the last bulk update was in late 2021, and it appears to have stopped there. The page
itself carries no update date.

## Licence — the key constraint

Conditions confirmed on the licence page:

- **Non-commercial prototyping and comp presentation only.** Commercial use (including use
  inside software or a product) requires explicit written permission.
- **No redistribution or repackaging** (the page states "do not repackage and distribute them
  yourself").
- Each manufacturer's trademark and brand guidelines apply **separately**.

In short, **they cannot be used in externally distributed marketing material.** Internal
comps, proposals and prototypes are the safe zone.

## Notes on use

- These are not Figma files. Meta's official Figma account (@meta) has only the Horizon OS UI
  kit, not the Devices kit.
- Coverage of older devices (feature phones, older Galaxy models, Lumia) is the real strength
  — those are hard to find elsewhere.
