# Samsung official device assets — inventory

**Samsung does not provide a mockup kit for design or marketing (confirmed absent).**
The only device-exterior asset available officially is the **Galaxy Emulator Skin** — a zip
that puts Galaxy device exteriors on the Android Studio emulator.

- URL: <https://developer.samsung.com/galaxy-emulator-skin>
- Format: zip (the emulator skin format — PNG layers plus a layout file)
- Purpose: development and testing. Using it as a comp mockup means the workaround of pulling
  the PNG layers out, and nothing anywhere says marketing use is permitted.

## Devices covered

Unlike the Apple kits, **it keeps up with current devices.** The Featured list at the time of
checking:

Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra · Z Fold8 · Z Flip8 · S26 Ultra · S26+ · S26 ·
**Z TriFold** · Tab S11 Ultra

Pages by category (each providing skins by generation):

| Category | Range confirmed |
|----------|-----------------|
| Galaxy S | the S10 series to the S26 series (including FE, Edge and Ultra variants) |
| Galaxy Z | Z Flip to Z Flip8 (including Flip7 FE) · Z Fold (formerly Galaxy Fold) to Z Fold8 Ultra · Z TriFold |
| Galaxy A | a separate page exists (device list unverified) |
| Galaxy Tab | a separate page exists — confirmed up to Tab S11 Ultra |
| Galaxy Note | a separate page exists (a discontinued line, effectively an archive) |

There is also an archive page (`/archive.html`) and separate release notes.

## Absent / unverified

- **Confirmed: no design mockup kit (PSD, Sketch, Figma)** — searching developer.samsung.com
  and Figma turns up no hardware mockup distribution.
- **The official Figma account @samsung exists but has zero resources** (confirmed
  2026-08-18).
- Press product images are on the Samsung Newsroom, but they are for press use and the terms
  for comp or marketing use are **unverified**.

## Notes on use

- The emulator skin is **a single front view**. There are no colour variants or side views.
- Comp work needing a Galaxy mockup effectively has to rely on third-party kits (for example
  the older Galaxy models in Meta Devices) or community files.
