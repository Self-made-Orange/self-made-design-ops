# Official Figma Community accounts — are there hardware mockups?

**Conclusion: no manufacturer or platform's official account has published a hardware mockup
kit on Figma.** The popular device mockup kits on Figma — including the "Apple Products UI
Kit" in [`apple-devices.md`](apple-devices.md) — are all published by third-party personal
accounts.

This is the result of opening each official account and checking its resource list
(2026-08-18).

## Results by official account

| Account | What it is | Hardware mockups | What is actually there |
|---------|------------|:---:|------------------------|
| [@apple](https://www.figma.com/@apple) | Apple official (since 2021) | ✗ | Apple Design Resources — UI kits for iOS/iPadOS 27, macOS 27, visionOS 26, watchOS 26, the App Icon Template and so on |

## The trap: third parties that look official

- **"Android UI Kit"** (community/file/1237551184114564748, 14k+ saves) — the publisher is a
  personal account, `ivyknight`. Not official. The official one is "🧰 Android UI Kit" on the
  @androiddesign account.
- The files that come up in searches for Pixel, Galaxy and Surface mockups (BRIX Templates
  and others) are all third party — each licence has to be checked individually.

## How to tell an official kit

1. Check that the publishing account handle is one of the official accounts in the table
   above.
2. Check that official documentation — developer.android.com, developer.apple.com and the
   like — **links directly** to that community file (the Wear OS design kit, for instance, is
   linked from developer.android.com/design/ui/wear).

## Note — Apple device bezels

Apple puts only UI kits on Figma, but it distributes product bezel images separately at
**developer.apple.com/design/resources/**. If you need devices newer than the third-party kit
in apple-devices.md (which stops at the iPhone 13), that is the official route.
(Outside the scope of this survey — a detailed inventory is unverified.)
