# Review report — the relocation planner dashboard (2026-08-18)

> **This is the first real application of the `agents/design-review.md` procedure.**
> Until now that guidance had never been run against an actual product (`TODO.md`, section D).
> What follows is **the review artefact** produced by following the procedure as written, and
> the defects found in the procedure itself have been folded into the items marked
> "(2026-08-18, reflecting the real run)" in `agents/design-review.md`.

## The target and the conditions of measurement

| item | value |
|------|-----|
| target | a private repository (address withheld) — the relocation planner |
| target revision | `74fab8e` (the shallow clone's HEAD, 2026-08-18) |
| files | `design-system/index.html` 492 lines (the catalogue) · `dashboard/index.html` 4,617 lines (the operational UI, 5 views) |

## Internal inconsistencies — eight

**The most valuable verdicts.** Things out of step within the product, independent of the
external sample.

| # | item | observed | corpus evidence | recommendation |
|---|------|--------|------------|------|
| I1 | button heights part at the same level of hierarchy | measured in the catalogue: `.button` **38** vs `.segmented`, `.preset-group` and `.chip-group button` **42** (radius 7 vs 8 too). | I2 | button ↔ input height mismatch | buttons 38 / inputs and selects 42 (measured; the select is 43.5, a fraction arising from derivation) | the "button ↔ input height alignment" part of the 78-sample re-synthesis in `patterns/form.md` — **about 30 fully shared** · 13 partly · **10 misaligned** (Cedar's 40 vs 38 is the same shape) | the majority practice is alignment. Pick 38 or 42 |
| I3 | radius token-literal drift | the dashboard's `:root` has **zero radius tokens** and eight literals (7px ×12 · 8 ×11 · 6 ×6 · 3 ×2 · 5 · 4 · 2 · 999). | I4 | the chart palette is a shadow of the semantic tokens | the six colours of the JS array (`dashboard/index.html`, line 3288) differ from the tokens by one or two digits — #3478f6/#3182f6 · **#00a874/#00a875** (a one-step difference in the blue channel) · #e5575f/#f04452 · #d39a1f/#d99000. | I5 | the same shadow geometry with different alphas | **within one dashboard file**, `0 12px 32px rgba(15,23,42,.22)` and `0 12px 32px rgb(15 23 42 / 16%)` coexist. The catalogue's `--shadow-float` token is `.16` | — (an internal fact about the product) | unify on `.16` and tokenise it |
| I6 | three focus-ring specs | the catalogue's global `3px / blue 26%` · the dashboard's `2px / blue 35%` · `.english-trigger`'s `outline:none` plus `box-shadow 0 0 0 3px / 24%`. ## What was not judged

- **z-index** — the dashboard uses 3, 8, 20, 30, 100 and 1000, and the catalogue 5, 10 and
  20. §1 of `design-review.md` says to flag "arbitrary numbers with no arithmetic", so it
  qualifies, but the file named as evidence is `systems/chakra-ui.md`, a single system, and
  **there is no cross-comparison document.**
  With no sample size to cite, no verdict is given and it is recorded here.
- **The primary button's fill colour** — the sample (`0905b88`) recorded a `var(--text)`
  (grey900) fill, while HEAD has a `var(--blue)` fill. Neither value is a departure in itself,
  so it is not a matter for a verdict but **a sample correction** (below).

## Questions (items that cannot be judged)

1. **What is the accessibility target level?** The product does not state it and the sample
   says `a11y_target: unverified`.
   If AA is not the target, the severity of E1–E4 changes.
2. **Where do `--green` #00a875 and `--yellow` #d99000 come from?** The question stands in the
   sample's "open questions" as it is. The sample records the yellow as "for text contrast on
   a light background", and the actual computed value against white is 2.64:1, short of AA.
3. **Is there a reason the six chart colours are kept apart from the semantic tokens?** If
   there is an intent, such as support for print or another medium, I4 becomes a matter for
   documentation rather than a finding.
4. **Is the catalogue premised on being a desktop-only document?** If so, E5 does not apply to
   the catalogue and only I8② remains.
5. **Was changing the primary button's fill from grey900 to blue an intended change?**

## What could not be checked

- **Post-render measurements of the dashboard's five views** — the `/api/bootstrap`
  authentication gate (which serves `/auth/pending.html` on a 403) means the DOM is not
  generated in a static clone. Every dashboard value is **measured from the CSS declarations
  plus box-model derivation**, and the actual rendered heights may differ.
- **Modals and toasts** — neither file has them (only three notice-banner levels). The
  `patterns/modal.md` (79 samples) and `feedback.md` axes have nothing to apply to.
- **Table sort UI and row selection** — the `patterns/table.md` axis. The catalogue's table has
  no sort or selection affordance, and the dashboard side could not be rendered.
- **A 390px measurement** — headless Chrome would not take the window width below 500px, so
  the measurement was made at 500px. The product's 390px breakpoint is unverified.
- **Whether the hit areas are reinforced (the dashboard)** — being unable to render it, no
  compensation beyond the visual size (such as a `::before` expansion) could be confirmed. The
  catalogue was confirmed to have none.

## An incidental finding — one error in the corpus itself

The "systems whose grid is not 4px" section of `tokens/scales.md` had three figures out of
step: **the heading said "six samples", the table had eight rows, and the body said "four of
the eight".** The "eight non-4px grid samples" cited by `agents/design-review.md` matched the
body.

**Handled (2026-08-19).** The heading was corrected to **eight samples**, matching the table
and the body, and **the fact that the eight rows are not all of one kind** was stated in the
section — Braid's grid is itself a multiple of 4px with only the notation unitless, and KRDS's
root is 10px but keeps the 4 family once converted to px. Excluding those two, **six samples
genuinely diverge from a 4px grid**, and four of them depart from 4, 8 and 16 at once. The
original "six samples" was **not a wrong number but a count of something else**, and the defect
was that it did not say which it was counting.
